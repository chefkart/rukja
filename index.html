FAQ

Sawaal? Jawab yahan hai.

Kya RukJa mera UPI PIN ya bank password maangta hai?+
Bilkul nahi. RukJa sirf aapke bank SMS padhta hai — jo pehle se aapke phone par hain. Koi password, koi credentials, koi OTP share karne ki zaroorat nahi. Saara data aapke phone par hi rehta hai — kuch bhi bahar nahi jaata.
RukJa kaunse banks aur UPI apps ke mandates detect karta hai?+
RukJa bank SMS scan karta hai — HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis, Kotak, PNB, aur 30+ other banks support karta hai. UPI app chahे GPay ho, PhonePe ho, ya Paytm — mandate detect hoga kyunki bank SMS bhejta hai, UPI app nahi.
Mandate cancel karne se kya subscription band ho jaata hai?+
Sirf automatic payment rukti hai — merchant ki service band nahi hoti. Jaise Hotstar ka UPI mandate cancel karo, toh Hotstar agle mahine apne aap nahi kaatega. Lekin Hotstar account tabhi band hoga jab aap unke app par seedha cancel karein. RukJa yeh baat cancel se pehle clearly batata hai.
RukJa ka subscription ₹39 kabhi bhi cancel ho sakta hai?+
Haan. Profile section mein anytime cancel kar sakte hain — koi penalty nahi, koi sawaal nahi. Current 28-day cycle khatam hone tak access milta rehega. Aur agar cancel karna bhi chaho toh pehle ek free month offer karte hain.
iOS par kab aayega?+
Abhi sirf Android. SMS reading ke liye Android zaroori hai — iOS par SMS access nahi milta same tarike se. iOS version ki planning hai — timeline announce karenge.
Kya RukJa offline bhi kaam karta hai?+
Haan. Mandate data phone par locally store hota hai. Internet na ho tab bhi saare mandates dekh sakte hain. Sync tab hota hai jab connection aata hai. 2G connections par bhi smoothly kaam karta hai.
